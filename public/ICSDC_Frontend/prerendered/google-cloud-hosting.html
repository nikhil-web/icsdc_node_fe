FAQs about ICSDC Google Cloud

01What makes ICSDC Google Cloud Hosting different from using Google Cloud directly?
With Google Cloud, you manage everything yourself - setup, security, backups, optimization, and troubleshooting. ICSDC adds full management, advanced security, performance tuning, backups, staging environments, human support, and an easy-to-use dashboard, making it ideal for businesses that want power without complexity.
02Will you handle the migration of my website or application?
Yes. We provide free, seamless migration with zero downtime. Our team plans, tests, and executes the migration to ensure your site or app transitions smoothly to Google Cloud.
03Do I need technical experience to use ICSDC's Google Cloud Hosting?
Not at all. Our platform is built for both technical and non-technical users. You get a simplified dashboard, one-click tools, automated backups, and full support from our experts.
04How secure is my website on ICSDC Google Cloud Hosting?
Your environment is protected with dedicated firewalls, continuous monitoring, malware scanning, DDoS protection, SSL certificates, and regular security hardening. We treat security as a top priority.
05Do you offer automated backups?
Yes. We provide daily automated backups with the option to take on-demand backups anytime. Restoring your website or application is as easy as one click.
06Can I host multiple websites or applications?
Yes. Depending on your plan, you can host multiple websites, staging environments, and applications on the same Google Cloud server, all fully managed by ICSDC.
07Is support available 24/7?
Absolutely. Our expert team is available 24/7/365 through chat and ticket support to assist with any technical issues or hosting queries.
08What performance benefits do I get with ICSDC?
You get server-level caching, CDN integration, optimized stack, NVMe SSD storage, tuned firewall rules, and real-time monitoring, all of which significantly improve speed, uptime, and reliability.
