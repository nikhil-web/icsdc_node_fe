FAQs about ICSDC Acronis Backup

01What is Acronis Backup?
Acronis Backup is a cloud-based solution that protects your files, servers, and systems by creating secure backups that can be restored when needed.
02How does ICSDC support Acronis Backup?
ICSDC provides the infrastructure, setup assistance, monitoring, and technical support needed to run Acronis Backup smoothly.
03Is my data encrypted during backup?
Yes. All backups are encrypted during transfer and while stored in the cloud to keep your data secure.
04Can I restore individual files or only full systems?
You can restore both - single files, folders, applications, or complete system images depending on your needs.
05Do I need technical experience to use Acronis Backup?
No. The interface is simple, and ICSDC helps with initial configuration so you can manage backups easily.
06How often are backups taken?
You can choose the backup frequency - daily, weekly, or custom schedules - based on your requirements.
07What happens if a backup fails?
The system alerts you automatically, and ICSDC's team can assist in identifying and resolving the issue.
08Can I increase my backup storage later?
Yes. Storage can be upgraded at any time without interrupting your existing backups.
09Does it protect against ransomware?
Yes. Acronis includes built-in ransomware detection and protection to prevent unauthorized changes to your data.
10Can I back up both physical and virtual machines?
Yes. Acronis Backup supports physical servers, virtual machines, cloud instances, and workstations.
