FAQs about Managed Dedicated Server

01What is a Fully Managed Dedicated Server?
A fully managed dedicated server is a hosting solution where ICSDC's team handles all technical tasks - setup, monitoring, security, optimization, updates, and support - so you don't have to manage the server yourself.
02How is a managed server different from a self-managed server?
In a self-managed server, you handle everything yourself. In a managed server, ICSDC's experts take care of all backend operations while you focus on your business.
03Do I need technical knowledge to use a managed dedicated server?
No. Managed servers are designed for businesses that want powerful hosting without dealing with technical maintenance or configurations.
04Can I customize the hardware or software on my server?
Yes. You can choose CPU, RAM, storage type, and operating system, and ICSDC will configure the server based on your needs.
05What kind of security does ICSDC provide on managed servers?
Managed servers include firewall setup, proactive monitoring, malware protection, DDoS mitigation, and regular security updates to safeguard your data.
06Will ICSDC handle backups and restoration?
Yes. Automated backups and easy restoration options are included to ensure your data remains safe and recoverable.
07What level of support can I expect?
You get 24/7 expert support for troubleshooting, performance tuning, and any technical issues related to your server.
08Can I upgrade my server resources later?
Absolutely. You can scale RAM, CPU, storage, and bandwidth anytime without downtime, ensuring your server grows with your business.
