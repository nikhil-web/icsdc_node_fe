Overview

ICSDC Consultants Pvt. Ltd. emphasizes data protection as a core commitment. The company states: "Your Data Security is Our Top Priority" and operates under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Personal Information Definition

The policy defines personal information broadly as "any factual or subjective information, recorded or not, about an identifiable individual," including age, name, ID numbers, income, ethnic origin, medical records, and opinions. Business contact details of employees are excluded.

Information Collection & Use

The organization collects data to:

  • Establish and maintain customer relationships
  • Deliver personalized services and marketing
  • Meet legal obligations

Ten Privacy Principles

1. Accountability

The company designates responsibility for compliance with privacy standards.

2. Identifying Purposes

Collection purposes are disclosed upfront.

3. Consent

Individual knowledge and approval required before collecting or disclosing data.

4. Limiting Collection

Only necessary information is gathered.

5. Limiting Use, Disclosure & Retention

Information restricted to consented purposes; third parties must maintain confidentiality; data retained only as long as needed.

6. Accuracy

Records kept current and verifiable.

7. Safeguards

Technical and procedural protections prevent unauthorized access.

8. Openness

Policies available 24/7 online.

9. Individual Access

Customers can request records, verify accuracy, and challenge information completeness with possible fee exceptions.

10. Challenging Compliance

Individuals may challenge ICSDC's compliance with the above principles, and ICSDC will investigate and respond to concerns.
