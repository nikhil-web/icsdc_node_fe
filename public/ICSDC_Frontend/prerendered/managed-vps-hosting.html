01What is Managed VPS Hosting?
Managed VPS Hosting means ICSDC handles all technical tasks — including server setup, security hardening, updates, backups, and monitoring — so you can focus entirely on your business instead of server maintenance.
02How is Managed VPS different from Self-Managed VPS?
With Managed VPS, ICSDC takes care of everything: updates, optimization, security, and support. With Self-Managed VPS, you manage the server on your own, including configurations, troubleshooting, and software setup.
03Will ICSDC help me migrate my website or application?
Yes. We provide end-to-end migration support with zero data loss and minimal downtime. Our engineers handle the entire process for a smooth transition.
04What kind of performance improvement can I expect?
With NVMe SSDs, dedicated resources, and optimized server tuning, most clients see faster load times, improved stability, and better performance during traffic spikes.
05Do I get full root access on a Managed VPS?
Yes. You retain complete control of your server environment, and ICSDC manages the backend operations and security simultaneously.
06How secure is ICSDC Managed VPS Hosting?
Your VPS includes DDoS protection, firewalls, security audits, malware scanning, and continuous threat monitoring to safeguard your applications and data.
07Can I scale my VPS resources later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade CPU, RAM, storage, and bandwidth anytime without downtime. Scaling is seamless and instant.
08What support do I get with my Managed VPS plan?
You get 24/7 access to hosting engineers for assistance with configurations, troubleshooting, tuning, migrations, and technical guidance — no bots, no scripted replies.
