Security

Your Digital Servers: Secured by ICSDC

Your dedicated server requires protection at every layer. We don't just provide tools; we deliver managed security services built and monitored by our experts to keep your data safe and your operations secure 24/7.

Firewall as a Service (FWaaS)

Intelligent security gate that filters traffic at the network level, blocking threats before they can reach your server hardware.

Privileged Access Management / Multi-Factor Authentication (PAM/MFA)

Secures your administrative layers and locks the master keys. MFA prevents access, even if a password is stolen.

Web Application Firewall (WAF)

A dedicated defense for your websites and applications, blocking application-layer attacks (like SQL injection and XSS).

SSL Certificates

Ensures all data transferred between the server and users is strongly encrypted, building customer trust.

FAQs about ICSDC Dedicated Server

01Do I receive full control over the server environment?
Yes. You get administrator access, allowing you to install any operating system, custom software, and configure all security settings exactly to your needs. This is your machine.
02How quickly will my dedicated server be ready to use?
We perform quick setup and complete testing to ensure your hardware is immediately stable and ready for deployment.
03Can ICSDC help me move my existing website or data?
Absolutely. If you choose a Managed server plan, our expert team provides free, assisted migration from your old host, ensuring a smooth and zero-downtime transfer.
04What if I need more resources (RAM, Storage) in the future?
Dedicated servers are highly scalable. You can easily upgrade components like RAM, hard drives (HDD/SSD), and even network bandwidth as your business grows, often with minimal service interruption.
05What is the difference between Managed and Unmanaged hosting?
In Unmanaged hosting, you handle all software, security, and updates. In Managed hosting, our ICSDC experts handle the patching, security hardening, and proactive monitoring for you.
