FAQs About ICSDC Shared Hosting

01What is Shared Hosting?
Shared Hosting is a type of web hosting where multiple websites share the same server resources. It’s perfect for small businesses, blogs, and startups that want reliable performance at an affordable cost.
02Is ICSDC Shared Hosting suitable for eCommerce websites?
Yes! ICSDC Shared Hosting is optimized for small to medium eCommerce sites. You’ll get fast page load speeds, free SSL, daily backups, and 99.9% uptime - everything an online store needs to stay secure and live.
03Can I upgrade my hosting plan later?
Absolutely. As your website grows, you can easily upgrade from Shared Hosting to VPS or Cloud Hosting without downtime or data loss. ICSDC makes scaling simple and seamless.
04Do you provide website migration support?
Yes, we do. Our team offers free website migration assistance to help you transfer your website from another provider to ICSDC safely and quickly, with zero technical hassle.
05What kind of support can I expect?
You’ll have access to 24/7 expert support from real engineers - not bots. Whether it’s setup, troubleshooting, or performance optimization, our team is always here to help.
06How many websites can I host on a shared hosting plan?
It depends on the plan you choose. Some plans support a single website, while higher-tier shared hosting plans let you host multiple websites under one account.
07Will shared hosting affect my website speed?
No - as long as your site fits the resource limits of your plan. ICSDC uses resource-isolated environments, SSD/NVMe storage, and optimized servers, ensuring stable speed even in shared environments.
08Do I need technical knowledge to use shared hosting?
Not at all. ICSDC Shared Hosting includes an easy control panel, one-click installs, guided setup, and support — making it beginner-friendly for anyone launching a website for the first time.
