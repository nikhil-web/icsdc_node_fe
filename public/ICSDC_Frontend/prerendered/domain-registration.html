FAQs about ICSDC Domain Registration

01How do I register a domain with ICSDC?
Search your desired name on our domain checker, choose your preferred extension, and complete the registration in a few clicks.
02Can I transfer my existing domain to ICSDC?
Yes, you can easily transfer your domain to ICSDC with zero downtime and full support throughout the process.
03Will my personal details be protected?
Absolutely. ICSDC offers WHOIS privacy protection to keep your personal information safe and hidden from public records.
04What if my desired domain name is already taken?
Try alternative extensions or variations — our smart search tool helps you find the closest available match instantly.
05Does ICSDC offer support after registration?
Yes! Our expert support team is available 24/7 to assist you with setup, renewals, DNS management, and more.
06How long does a domain registration last?
Most domains can be registered for 1 year, and you can extend them for multiple years. Some extensions may require a longer minimum term.
07Can I change my domain after registering?
No, domain names can’t be renamed. You can register a new one and redirect your old domain to it.
08What are TLDs, ccTLDs, and gTLDs?
TLD: The ending of a domain (e.g., .com) ccTLD: Country-specific TLDs (e.g., .in, .uk) gTLD: General TLDs like .com, .net, .online, etc.
09What if the name I want is unavailable?
Try a different extension or a slight variation of your name. Our domain search will also suggest available alternatives.
