FAQs about ICSDC Azure Cloud

01Why should I choose ICSDC instead of buying Azure directly?
When you buy Azure directly, you're responsible for setup, management, security, and optimisation. With ICSDC, you get the full Azure platform plus expert engineering, continuous monitoring and support - so you focus on your business, not maintaining cloud infrastructure.
02Will I get the same Azure features if I go through ICSDC?
Yes - you receive the same Microsoft Azure services. The difference is that ICSDC layers on tailored architecture, cost control, and human support, so you use those features more effectively and with less effort.
03Do you manage everything end-to-end?
Yes. From planning and migration to ongoing management, backups, scaling and optimisation - we handle the full lifecycle of your Azure cloud, letting you avoid the complexity of doing it yourself.
04Can you reduce my existing Azure bill?
Definitely. We assess your usage, identify inefficiencies, and apply best-practices like right-sizing, reserved instances and automation. Most clients see meaningful savings without compromising performance.
05Is support from ICSDC better than Microsoft support?
Microsoft offers strong platform support, but ICSDC adds personalised service - direct access to engineers familiar with your environment, faster responses, and proactive issue resolution instead of reactive tickets.
06Will ICSDC help during future expansions?
Yes. As your business grows, we will scale your environment, deploy new services, optimise for performance and cost, and adapt your Azure setup - all so you're ready for growth without re-architecting everything.
07Can I migrate from my current hosting to Azure through ICSDC?
Yes. We plan and execute the migration step-by-step, taking care of data transfer, configuration, validation and cut-over - with minimal impact to your users and business operations.
08What if something breaks at night?
You're covered 24×7. Our monitoring and engineering team are always active, so you don't have to rely solely on Microsoft's standard support windows. We handle issues immediately and keep your operations running.
