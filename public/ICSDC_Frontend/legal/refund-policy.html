      Refund Eligibility

      To qualify for a refund, customers must:

      • Submit requests within "[number of days] days of your purchase"
      • Ensure the service is covered by the refund policy
      • Provide reasons falling within acceptable criteria

      Refund Process

      Customers should "contact our customer support team at [support email or phone number]" and provide purchase details and refund justification.

      Refund Criteria

      Refunds may be issued when:

      • "The service purchased is found to be defective or not as described"
      • The service is canceled within the specified cancellation period
      • Billing errors result in overcharges

      Non-Refundable Items

      The following are typically ineligible:

      • "Services that have already been rendered or consumed"
      • Customized or personalized services
      • "Services provided on a subscription basis"
