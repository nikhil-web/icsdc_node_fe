      3.7 Email Abuse

      The following activities are prohibited email abuse:

      1. Unsolicited Bulk Emailing: All accounts, websites, and database applications/scripts cannot be used for mass emailing, including commercial advertising, mass mailing exceeding 1000 SMTP emails per hour per email account, informational announcements, charity requests, petitions, and political/religious content. Such emails may only be sent if the receiver explicitly requested them.
      2. Stealth Emailing: Forging, altering, or removing email headers.
      3. Mail Bombing: Sending large email numbers to disrupt or compromise servers, systems, or networks.
